Do you know whether ChatGPT recommends your company when someone asks for the best option in your category? Most people don't — only 16% of brands track AI search performance systematically ([source](https://ecommerceguide.com/a/ecommerce-statistics/)), even though ChatGPT alone now sends 92.4% of all trackable AI referral traffic, up 12.8x in 19 months ([source](https://somethinginc.com/blog/ai-referral-traffic-chatgpt-92-percent/)).

## Watching one AI engine isn't enough anymore

Q2 2026 data across the top 1,000 online retailers found the number with ChatGPT as their biggest AI traffic source fell from 844 to 722 in one quarter, while Gemini grew from 16 to 32 retailers, Perplexity from 8 to 21, and Claude from 1 to 15 ([source](https://stellagent.ai/insights/ai-referral-traffic-sources-online-retailers-q2-2026)).

## More mentions doesn't automatically mean more revenue

ChatGPT accounts for roughly 97% of LLM-referred ecommerce traffic by volume, but Perplexity-referred visits convert at a 57% higher average order value ([source](https://ecommerceguide.com/a/ecommerce-statistics/)). And 68% of AI citations pull from third-party sources rather than a company's own website.

## What tracking this actually looks like

Pick a handful of questions a real customer might ask an AI tool, run them weekly across a few engines, and note whether you're mentioned, cited, or absent — and who shows up instead.

## Why this matters more for smaller companies, not less

A large, well-known brand gets mentioned by AI tools regardless of whether anyone is watching. A smaller company is exactly the one that gets left out of the answer entirely if nobody is tracking it.

## FAQ

**How is this different from Google rank tracking?** Rank tracking measures position on a results page; this measures whether you're mentioned or cited inside a generated answer at all.

**How often should we check?** Weekly at minimum.

**Do we need expensive tools to start?** No — a manual check across a handful of questions, run consistently, is a legitimate start.

**What do we do if we're not mentioned?** Look at who is cited instead — that tells you which sites are worth a presence on.

**Is this only relevant for ecommerce?** No — the dynamic applies to B2B and services businesses too.
