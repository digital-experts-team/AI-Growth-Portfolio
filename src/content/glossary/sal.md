## Why it matters for B2B

SAL serves as the functional contract between marketing demand generation and sales execution. Without a defined SAL stage, sales reps either ignore leads passed by marketing or waste hours investigating incomplete records.

Enforcing SAL status requires automated validation: checking that work emails are verified, company firmographics fit ICP thresholds, and no open deal already exists. When automated enrichment resolves verified fields, SAL acceptance rates improve significantly.
