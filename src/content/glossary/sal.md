<AnswerBlock question="What is a Sales Accepted Lead (SAL)?">
A Sales Accepted Lead (SAL) is an MQL that has been reviewed and formally accepted by sales or an SDR team for active outreach. SAL status verifies that the contact data is complete, verified, and not in conflict with existing accounts, serving as the critical service level agreement checkpoint between marketing and sales.
</AnswerBlock>

## The RevOps Bridge

SAL serves as the contract between marketing generation and sales execution. When automated waterfall enrichment resolves accurate email, phone, and ICP fields, the SAL acceptance rate rises above 90%.
