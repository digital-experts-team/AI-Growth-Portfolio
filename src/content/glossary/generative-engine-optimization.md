## What GEO work involves

- A consistent entity: the same name, description and facts on your site, LinkedIn and other profiles, linked with `sameAs` schema.
- Specific, verifiable claims and data that a model can cite.
- Clear structure and an `llms.txt` file that points AI tools to your key pages.
- Allowing AI crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended) in robots.txt.
