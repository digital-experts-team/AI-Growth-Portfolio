## Why AEO matters for B2B

Buyers ask AI assistants and search engines direct questions. The page that gives the clearest, most quotable answer tends to be used, and that is where AEO work focuses.

## What AEO work involves

- Server-rendered or static pages so the content is in the HTML.
- Question-style headings with a short direct answer underneath.
- Schema markup (FAQPage, HowTo, DefinedTerm, Article) that matches the visible content.
- Consistent names and facts about your company across your site and profiles.
