## Why it matters for B2B

Blasting generic contact lists burns domain deliverability and generates low response rates. Signal-based selling allows lean sales teams to focus outbound energy on accounts currently experiencing active triggers or organizational changes.

By monitoring key events—such as pricing page visits, SDR hiring spikes, funding announcements, and technology changes—revenue teams reach prospects with contextually relevant messaging when receptivity is highest.

## Core Signals to Monitor

- **Website Intent**: Dwell time on pricing or technical comparison pages.
- **Organizational Growth**: Rapid hiring of sales reps or engineers.
- **Capital Infusion**: Capital raises or expansion announcements.
