## Why it matters for B2B

SQLs represent high-probability pipeline opportunities. When a prospect achieves SQL status, sales executives can invest time in tailored discovery and solution demos knowing that account firmographics and buying authority have been programmatically verified.

A modern GTM architecture validates SQLs using automated enrichment and intent scoring. When an account meets the fit matrix and intent threshold, it is automatically routed to the correct account executive with complete session history.
