<AnswerBlock question="What is a Sales Qualified Lead (SQL)?">
A Sales Qualified Lead (SQL) is a vetted prospective buyer that has met explicit ICP requirements, demonstrated high buying intent, and been accepted by sales as ready for discovery or demo. In automated GTM engines, SQL status triggers immediate CRM opportunity creation, automated rep calendar booking, and strict speed-to-lead follow-up SLAs.
</AnswerBlock>
