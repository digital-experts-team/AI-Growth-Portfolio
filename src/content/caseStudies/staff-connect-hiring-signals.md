## The problem

The GCC IT staffing team spent time checking job boards and LinkedIn for potential mandates. It needed a repeatable way to spot relevant hiring activity and get qualified opportunities to the right person.

## What I built

I helped build and run a system that monitored long-open vacancies, contract wins, and regional expansion. It enriched employer accounts, scored them for fit, and routed qualified opportunities to a recruiter or business development queue. A separate inbound campaign fed into the same qualification process.

## How the team used it

Recruiters and BD reps received employer accounts with the signal that made each one worth investigating. Both inbound and outbound opportunities went through the same qualification rules, giving the team one process for deciding what to pursue and who should own it.

## Workflow

1. Capture a hiring signal or inbound enquiry.
2. Enrich the employer account.
3. Qualify the opportunity.
4. Route it to the recruiter or BD queue for follow-up.

Setup took two weeks.
