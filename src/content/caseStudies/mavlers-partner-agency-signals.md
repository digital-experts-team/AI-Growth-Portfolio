## The problem

Website activity gave sales an early signal of interest. The team needed company context and a consistent way to decide which accounts represented potential agency partners worth following up with.

## What I built

I led an ICP workshop and translated the findings into account segments, scoring rules, and follow-up sequences. I built a SalesIQ-to-HubSpot workflow that combined website activity, company data, and engagement. I mapped qualification to the MQL and SQL stages the team already used in HubSpot.

## How sales used it

Qualified accounts entered the team's existing sales process. Reps could see which company showed interest, why it fit, and which follow-up sequence applied.

## Workflow

1. Capture website activity in SalesIQ.
2. Add company context and engagement data.
3. Score the account against the ICP.
4. Qualify it in HubSpot using the team's lifecycle stages.
5. Start the appropriate sales follow-up.
