## What a RevOps consultant fixes

If any of these sound familiar, the problem is usually the system, not the reps:

- Sales ignores marketing's leads because nobody agreed what an MQL is.
- High-intent visitors and inbound leads sit unassigned for days.
- CRM fields are half empty, so scoring and reporting can't be trusted.
- Every new tool (Clay, Apollo, a website visitor tool) was connected once and never maintained.
- Marketing reports leads, sales reports pipeline, and the numbers never match.

## How an engagement works

1. **Audit (first weeks):** lifecycle stages, scoring, routing, enrichment coverage and data hygiene. You get a written list of what's broken and what to fix first.
2. **Build:** the highest-impact fix first, usually MQL definitions plus scoring and routing, shipped behind a human review step.
3. **Document and hand over:** every workflow documented so your team can run and change it.
4. **Improve:** review MQL to SQL conversion and adjust weights and rules.
